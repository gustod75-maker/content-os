What it is

CONTENT OS is a private desktop application, running only on its owner's own computer, used to manage the short educational videos of the channel « Pourquoi ? Dossier n° » (one everyday "why" question answered per video). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Plans the publication calendar of the owner's own videos.
  • Publishes a video to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts, at the time the owner chose, only after the owner explicitly validates it.
  • Reads the public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to follow their performance.

Platforms

Windows desktop application. Data is stored only on the owner's computer; access tokens are encrypted locally and never sent to any third-party server.
