Privacy Policy

CONTENT OSTerms of Service

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Data processed

Account identifier and display name, avatar, follower count, the list of the account's own published videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares), provided by the platform with the account holder's consent.

Purpose

Following the performance of the owner's own videos and organizing their publication. No other use.

Storage

Data is stored only on the owner's computer. Access tokens are encrypted locally. No data is sent to a third-party server, sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Video files

When the owner validates a publication, the video file is sent directly from the owner's computer to the owner's own account on the platform.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings. Statistics stored on the owner's computer can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

The owner of the tool, through the contact address of the TikTok for Developers account of the application.
